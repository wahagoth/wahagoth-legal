This Privacy Policy explains what information Waha - goth ("Waha", "we", "us") handles when you use the Waha - goth iOS app (the "App"), why it is used, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. By using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Contact: wahagoth@gmail.com

1. Who can use Waha

Waha is a community for adult Gothic, Lolita, and cosplay creators. The App is for people aged 18 or older. Before Quick Start, you must confirm that you are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly handle information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using the App, contact us and we will remove the related content.

2. Information we handle

2.1 Account information

  • Quick Start account. When you tap Quick Start, the App creates a random username and password for your account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or social login to create an account.
  • Onboarding choices. Your craft role (Model, Makeup Artist, Wig Stylist, or Costume Designer) and your aesthetic preference.
  • Age confirmation. A record that you confirmed you are 18 or older.

2.2 Profile information you choose to add

Display name, handle, bio, portfolio link, atelier link, location text, and an optional profile portrait. Location is free text you type; the App does not read your device's GPS location.

2.3 Content you create

  • Lookbook posts (title, story text, and an optional photo or short clip)
  • Comments on posts
  • Direct messages, including text, photos, and voice notes sent in chats
  • Chat requests you send, accept, or decline
  • Likes, saves, and Boosts you give to lookbooks
  • AI lookbook renders you create in the Roles studio, and the try-on photos you add there (see Section 5)

2.4 Safety records

  • Accounts you block
  • Posts and comments you hide or report, the reason you selected, and the time of the report (used to prevent duplicate reports of the same item within 24 hours)

2.5 Purchase information

  • Your Iron balance and the history of Iron credited from purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We never receive or store your card number, billing address, or Apple Account password. The App receives a transaction confirmation from Apple's StoreKit so it can credit Iron.

2.6 Information we do not collect

We do not use advertising identifiers, third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or cross-app tracking. We do not read your contacts, calendar, health data, or precise location.

4. Facial data

4.1 What we collect

Waha does not collect facial geometry, faceprints, face templates, or any other biometric identifiers. The App does not use Face ID data, the TrueDepth camera's depth map, ARKit face tracking, or face recognition of any kind.

Photos you choose to use as a profile portrait, attach to a lookbook post, send in a chat, or add as a try-on photo in the Roles studio may show your face or another person's face. The App treats these photos only as ordinary images that you selected.

4.2 How we use it

These photos are used only to display the content you chose to share: your portrait on your profile, your posts, and your comments and messages; your lookbook posts in the Circles feed and post detail; and the photos inside the chat where you sent them. Try-on photos are used only to create the AI render you request (Section 5). They are never analyzed to detect, recognize, or measure faces, and they are not used for advertising or profiling.

4.3 Sharing and where it is stored

Photos that may show a face are kept in the App's storage on your iPhone. The only exception is a photo you add yourself to the Try-On References in the Roles studio: when you tap Generate Outfit Visual, that photo is sent to Agnes AI, the third-party AI service described in Section 5, so it can create your render. Your portrait, post photos, and chat photos are never sent to Agnes AI. No photo is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

4.4 Retention

These photos are kept until you delete the post that contains them, replace your portrait, or delete your account. Try-on photos are not saved to storage by the App. They are held only while the Roles studio is open and are cleared when you remove them or close the App. Delete Account (Me > Account Actions > Delete Account) permanently erases every portrait, post photo, and chat photo stored by the App.

This Section 4 is our full explanation of how the App collects, uses, discloses, shares, and retains facial data and photos that may show a face.

5. AI image generation (third-party AI)

This section explains the only AI feature in the App and exactly what it sends, receives, and keeps.

5.1 What the feature does

The Roles studio has a Generate Outfit Visual button that creates a new fashion lookbook image from a written outfit recipe and, if you choose, up to two photos you add to Try-On References. The image is generated by Agnes AI (apihub.agnes-ai.com), a third-party image-generation service. Waha does not run any other AI, machine-learning, or automated analysis on you or your content.

5.2 When data is sent

Data is sent to Agnes AI only when you tap Generate Outfit Visual. Nothing is sent in the background, on launch, when you browse, post, comment, or chat, or when you use any other feature. If you never tap Generate Outfit Visual, nothing is ever sent to Agnes AI. Each render costs 30 Iron, so every request comes from an action you take on purpose.

5.3 Exactly what is sent

Each render request contains only:

Item Source
Outfit recipe text The words you type in the recipe box
Style options The archetype, material, craft focus, and framing (3:4, 1:1, or 9:16) you select in the studio
Fixed style instructions Text written by Waha, such as "photoreal fashion lookbook, editorial lighting, no text, no watermark"
Reference images Either the sample style image that ships inside the App for the archetype you selected, or the photos you added to Try-On References: Slot 1 (Outfit Look, the clothing to copy) and Slot 2 (Model Photo, the person who wears it). Photos are resized to at most 1024 pixels on the long side
Output settings The model name, image size, and response format

5.4 Try-on photos

Try-On References are optional. If you add a photo, the App shows a notice the first time you generate with photos. It names Agnes AI, explains what is sent, and asks you to agree before anything is sent. If you choose Not Now, nothing is sent. A try-on photo may show a face. Waha does not create facial geometry, faceprints, or any biometric data from it, and does not use it to identify anyone. The photo is sent only to create the render you asked for.

5.5 What is never sent

The App does not send Agnes AI any of the following:

  • Your clips, voice notes, or any photo other than the try-on photos you add yourself
  • Your profile portrait, post photos, or chat photos
  • Facial data, biometric data, or face measurements of any kind
  • Your username, password, display name, handle, bio, links, or location text
  • Your posts, comments, chats, or chat requests
  • Your Iron balance, purchase history, or Apple Account information
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, contacts, or precise location
  • Cookies or any account token that identifies you

The request is made with a temporary network session that does not store cookies or a cache. Like any internet request, the connection itself reaches Agnes AI's servers from your network (for example, an IP address), which is needed to return the image. Waha does not attach any identifier that links a request to you or to your Waha account.

5.6 Your recipe text and try-on photos

The recipe and the try-on photos are the only parts of a request that you provide. Only add try-on photos of yourself, or of an adult who has agreed to it. Do not add photos of minors. Please do not include personal information, real names, contact details, or descriptions of identifiable real people in a recipe. Before a recipe is sent, the App checks it against our banned-phrase list. Recipes that match are blocked and are not sent.

5.7 What comes back and where it is kept

Agnes AI returns one generated image. The App downloads it and saves it in Recent Lab Renders in the Roles studio, with a short title taken from your recipe. Renders are kept in the App's storage on your device. They are not posted to the Circles feed or shown to other users unless you choose to share them yourself. If a render fails, nothing is saved and your 30 Iron is returned.

5.8 Retention

Waha does not keep a copy of your recipe or render anywhere other than your Recent Lab Renders, and does not keep try-on photos after the request. You can delete a single render from its viewer, and all renders are erased when you delete your account. Agnes AI processes each request, including any try-on photos, under its own terms and privacy policy. Waha does not send your account details or any identifier with a request, so a request cannot be linked to your Waha account.

5.9 No other AI processing

Waha does not use AI to analyze, tag, recognize, or score your photos, your face, your messages, or your behavior. Waha does not use AI to make automated decisions about your account. Content moderation in Waha uses the banned-phrase list, reports, and blocks described in Section 7. It does not use AI.

5.10 Your choices

Using AI image generation and try-on photos is optional. Every other feature works without them. You can delete your account at any time to erase every render. Questions about this feature: wahagoth@gmail.com.

6. How information is stored

Your account details, profile, posts, comments, messages, chat requests, safety records, Iron balance, and renders are saved with your account in the App's storage on your device. Photos, clips, and voice notes you add are saved in the App's media folder on your device. We use this information only to run the features described in this Policy.

7. Community safety and reports

  • Filtering. Posts, comments, and AI recipes are checked against a list of banned phrases before they are published or sent. Text that matches is blocked and you are asked to change it.
  • Reporting. You can report a post from the feed menu or post detail, a comment from post detail, a lookbook from the Roles screen, and a user from a chat. A report immediately hides the reported item for you. By default, the report also blocks the author.
  • Blocking. Blocking hides the author's posts, comments, chat threads, and chat requests from you. You can review and unblock accounts in Me > Blocked Accounts.
  • Contacting moderation. To reach our moderation team about abusive content or users, email wahagoth@gmail.com with the handle and a short description. We act on these reports within 24 hours (see the Terms of Service).

The report reason and time are kept as a safety record so the App can prevent duplicate reports of the same item within 24 hours.

8. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only:

  • With Apple, to process purchases you make through the App Store
  • With Agnes AI, limited to the render request described in Section 5

  • If required by law, or to protect the safety of users

9. Retention and deletion

  • Sign Out ends your session only. Your account, content, and Iron balance stay on the device, and Quick Start signs you back into the same account.
  • Delete Account (Me > Account Actions > Delete Account, then type DELETE to confirm) permanently erases your username and password, profile, portrait, posts, comments, messages, chat requests, safety records, Iron balance, renders, and all photos, clips, and voice notes stored by the App. This cannot be undone.
  • Unused Iron is lost when you delete your account and is not refunded.
  • You can also delete individual posts you created at any time.

If you need help deleting information, email wahagoth@gmail.com.
