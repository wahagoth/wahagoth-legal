6. AI lookbook renders

6.1 How it works

Generate Outfit Visual in the Roles studio creates a new fashion image using Agnes AI, a third-party image-generation service. A request is sent only when you tap the button. It contains your recipe text, the style options you select, fixed style instructions written by Waha, and either a sample reference image that ships with the App or the try-on photos you add yourself (Slot 1 Outfit Look and Slot 2 Model Photo). It never includes your portrait, post or chat photos, your account details, or any identifier. The full list is in Section 5 of the Privacy Policy.

6.2 Iron

Each render costs 30 Iron. If a render does not complete, the 30 Iron is returned to your balance.

6.3 Rules for recipes and try-on photos

Section 4 applies to every recipe and try-on photo. You may add a try-on photo only of yourself, or of an adult who has agreed to have their photo used this way. By adding a photo you confirm that you have this permission. In addition, you must not use AI renders to:
